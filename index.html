How to find your growth constraint

  1. Enter your real numbers. Set each slider to your current funnel metrics, then hit “Save these as my baseline.”
  2. Watch Monthly Net Profit. If it’s red, an ad gets you a client but the unit economics don’t pay — something upstream is the constraint.
  3. Find the amber sliders. A slider glows green when it hits a healthy target (Show ≥ 70%, Close ≥ 18%, Churn ≤ 10%, CPQBC ≤ $350, Calls > 30). The ones still amber are your candidates.
  4. Solve one at a time. Drag a single amber slider into its green zone. The one that flips net profit positive — or moves it most — is the constraint to fix first.
  5. Check the price. The Recommended Price Point shows the monthly price needed to hit your target LGP:CAC at today’s numbers.
