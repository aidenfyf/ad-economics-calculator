How to use this

  1. Set your numbers. Fill in the Offer & Target fields, then drag each slider to match your agency’s real funnel. Nothing here is assumed; the model only knows what you tell it.
  2. Watch Monthly Net Profit. If it shows red, your ads win clients but the unit economics don’t pay yet. A constraint is capping you.
  3. Spot the amber sliders. A slider turns green once it hits a healthy target (Show ≥ 70%, Close ≥ 18%, Churn ≤ 10%, CPQBC ≤ $350, Calls > 30). The amber ones are your suspects.
  4. Solve one at a time. Move a single amber slider into its green zone. Whichever one flips net profit positive, or moves it the most, is the constraint to fix first.
  5. Check the price. The Recommended Price Point shows the monthly price you’d need to hit your target LGP:CAC at your current numbers.
