# Ad Constraint Calculator

Static single-page tool (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build) deployed on Vercel.
Repo: aidenfyf/ad-economics-calculator · Live: https://ad-economics-calculator.vercel.app

## Design Context

### Users
AI-agency owners and their account managers / growth partners. They run paid ads
to acquire clients and need to find the single constraint capping profitable
growth. Numerate operators who think in CAC, LTGP, ROAS, churn. Used at a desk
during focused modeling sessions and in weekly client calls — not casual browsing.

### Brand Personality
Blunt, exacting, profit-first. Hormozi-influenced. "Numbers don't lie." Confident
and a little swaggering, but grounded entirely in math. Three words: **exacting,
blunt, operator-grade**. Not corporate-soft, not playful, not cute.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern and elevated, and unmistakably human-designed — must NOT read as generic
AI output. Dark, instrument/cockpit feel for focused work. Signature warm **amber**
accent with **green = healthy** and **red = profit leak** status semantics.
Avoid purple, pink, cyan-on-dark, neon gradients, and the Inter/AI-default look.
Distinctive grotesque display type + monospaced numerals for a precise, financial-
instrument read.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display/headings), Hanken Grotesk (body/UI/labels),
Spline Sans Mono (all metric numbers and slider readouts). Never Inter.

### Design Principles
1. **One metric leads.** LTGP:CAC and the constraint diagnosis are the hero;
   every other number supports them. Hierarchy must reflect that.
2. **Numbers are the interface.** Tabular, monospaced, precise. The data carries
   the design — not decoration.
3. **Blunt clarity over decoration.** Every element earns its place. Remove
   anything that doesn't move the diagnosis forward.
4. **Color is a signal, used sparingly.** Amber = attention/brand, green =
   healthy, red = leak. No color for its own sake. (60/30/10 weighting.)
5. **Elevated restraint.** Distinctive type, warm-tinted neutrals, and tight
   spatial rhythm do the work — not effects, glows, or glassmorphism.

### Theme
Dark, derived from context: focused operator tool used at a desk/evening, cockpit
identity, builds on the approved direction. Warm-tinted (neutrals lean toward the
amber hue), never pure black or pure white.
